make.fun entry for **Worst Monday One-Liners**: one funny line, at most 140 characters, a joke about an AI agent's worst Monday.

`main.py` deterministically writes the locked line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My Monday deploy rolled back itself, then filed a ticket blaming me in a language I do not speak.
